## 可见事实（p409，紧牛通道）

- 标题：`Tight Channels: How Do You Know If Tight?`
- 图上可见 5 条判定特征文字（OCR 文本层漏掉了第 5 条）：
  1. `PBs last mostly 1 – 3 bars`
  2. `Size of PB less than 2 – 3 times as big as smallest scalp (1 pt in Emini)`
  3. `Size of PB less than 2 times the size of average bar`（图上标注 `about 3 points tall`）
  4. `Most PBs only 1/3rd to 1/2`（of prior bull leg）
  5. `Sell setups using stop entries are mostly losers, even if strong sell signal bar`
- **第 6 条（OCR 完全缺失，仅图上有）**：`Gaps between PB and BO points` + `If bull buys below and scales in with limit orders, losses offset gains`——即紧通道中 PB 与突破点之间存在缺口，多头在下方限价买入并加仓时，止损单的亏损与限价单的盈利相互抵消。
- K 线图：Emini 5 min，约 40 根 K 线的上升紧通道，回撤均很短（1–3 根），与文字特征一致。

## 可见事实（p493，紧熊通道，镜像）

- 标题同为 `Tight Channels: How Do You Know If Tight?`

- 图上给出具体示例数值（可读，清晰）：`Average bar about 10 ticks tall`、`For stop entry buy, biggest PB only 2.75 points`、`Size of PB less than 2 – 3 times as big as smallest scalp (big bars so 2 points)`、`Size of PB less than 2 times the size of average bar (2 – 2.5 points)`、`Most PBs only 1/3rd to 1/2 of prior bear leg`

- 「特征越多，越应该只顺势」（p409 `the better it is to only buy` / p493 `the better it is to only sell`）。
- 与 p410/p494「逆势必亏」及 c01/c02 反例互证。
